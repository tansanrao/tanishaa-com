## Overview

Ébon is a modern skincare brand centered around the natural benefits of clay. The collection features a range of pure, organic clay masks and ritual-based treatments designed to cleanse, detoxify, and restore the skin.

## Process

The visual identity for Ébon was inspired by the brand's hero ingredient—activated charcoal. Rather than using traditional product photography or abstract graphics, I incorporated an original charcoal sketch as the primary packaging element. The organic textures and expressive marks reflect the raw, natural qualities of clay while creating a distinctive visual identity.
