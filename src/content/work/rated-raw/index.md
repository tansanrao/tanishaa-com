## Overview

For my motion graphics class, I created *Rated Raw*, a fictional production company and streaming platform centered around adult animation. All animation for the project was hand-drawn in Procreate and later compiled, animated, and edited in Adobe After Effects to create the final sizzle reel.

*Created as a student motion graphics project. Audio featured in this reel is copyrighted material owned by its respective creators and is used solely for non-commercial educational purposes.*

## Process

The concept for *Rated Raw* was kind of always there, but I wasn’t completely sure what visual direction or animation style I wanted to use at first. A lot of the development happened naturally through experimentation. Most of the drawings were created during a few sleepless nights, when it all came together.
